## What happened to Mealime

According to [Mealime's closing notice](https://www.mealime.com/closing):

- The app was discontinued on **21 October 2026**.
- Mealime's recipes have moved to **Meals Hub**, part of the grocery apps run by Albertsons Companies: Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco and other US supermarket chains.
- Paid subscriptions were wound down, and Pro features were free for everyone in the final weeks.
- Your account doesn't move across automatically. You sign up to an Albertsons Companies app and set your preferences again.
- The notice said all personal data would be deleted when the app shut down, and didn't mention any way to export your plans or saved recipes.

## Keep the Mealime recipes you like

Mealime's recipes are also published as web pages, and they were still online on 23 October 2026. You can bring any you want to keep into BiteSync before they're taken down:

1. Open the recipe on mealime.com and copy the link.
2. In BiteSync, go to **Recipes**, tap **Import** (**Import from URL** on a computer) and paste it. The ingredients and method come across, and the nutrition is calculated from the ingredients.
3. Check the quantities before saving. The importer converts cups and pounds to grams, and packet sizes, such as a "4 oz pack" of feta, sometimes need correcting.

## Other options

- **Meals Hub:** the natural move if you shop at Safeway, Albertsons, Vons or another Albertsons-group store.
- **[Paprika](https://www.paprikaapp.com/):** a recipe manager with week or month meal plans and an aisle-sorted grocery list. It runs on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, and each version is sold separately. You choose and place the recipes yourself; it doesn't plan to calorie or protein targets.
- **[Plan to Eat](https://www.plantoeat.com/):** saves recipes from websites onto a meal-planning calendar that fills in a shopping list. It costs $5.95 a month or $49 a year after a 14-day trial. Again, you place the recipes yourself, with no planning to targets.
