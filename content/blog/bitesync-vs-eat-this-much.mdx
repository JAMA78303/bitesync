Eat This Much and BiteSync both plan meals around your calorie and macro targets, so they're closer than most apps. There are three real differences:

- **Whose recipes the plan uses.** Eat This Much builds plans from its own database of recipes and foods. BiteSync builds the week from *your* recipes: the ones you add, import or have written for you.
- **What's free.** Eat This Much's free plan generates a day at a time, and weekly plans and the grocery list are on Premium. BiteSync's free plan has the weekly planner and grocery list, but you place the meals yourself. Automatic weekly plans are on Pro.
- **How vitamins and minerals are judged.** Both apps track them. BiteSync checks 12 across the whole week against reference values that differ for men and women, and feeds any gap into the next plan.

*BiteSync is our app. We've tried to be fair about where Eat This Much is the better choice.*

## Where Eat This Much is stronger

- **Automatic plans for free.** The free plan generates daily meal plans to your targets. On BiteSync's free plan, you choose and place the meals; the AI only fills the week for you on Pro.
- **A ready-made recipe database.** You don't need any recipes of your own to start. BiteSync starts you with ten recipes built to your targets and grows from there.
- **Barcodes and apps.** You can add foods by scanning barcodes, and there are iPhone and Android apps. BiteSync has no barcode scanner and runs in the browser.
- **Extras on Premium.** Automatic leftovers, grocery delivery integration, saved favourite weeks and PDF meal plans you can share.

## Where BiteSync is stronger

- **Your own food.** The plan is built from the meals you actually cook, including recipes imported from any recipe site by pasting the link. Many people find that easier to stick to than a week of unfamiliar dishes.
- **Weekly planning and grocery list on the free plan.** Eat This Much puts both on Premium. BiteSync's list merges the whole week and leaves off what's already in your pantry.
- **Vitamins and minerals across the week.** BiteSync Pro checks 12, such as iron, iodine and vitamin D, against published reference values split by sex. That matters: a woman of reproductive age needs about 70% more iron than a man. It judges the whole week, which is how adequacy actually works, and gaps feed back into the next plan.
