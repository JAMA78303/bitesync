Samsung Food and BiteSync both plan your meals and turn the plan into a shopping list. The difference is what the plan is built around. Samsung Food is built around **recipes**: save them from anywhere, plan them by day or week, share with your household. All of that is free. BiteSync is built around **targets**: every day of the week is sized to your calories and protein before you shop. If you want a free, flexible recipe organiser, choose Samsung Food. If hitting your numbers is the point, choose BiteSync.

*BiteSync is our app. We've tried to be fair about where Samsung Food is the better choice.*

## Where Samsung Food is stronger

- **More for free.** Saving recipes from the web, daily and weekly planning, a shopping list from the plan (sortable by aisle or recipe), and sharing with family all cost nothing. BiteSync's free plan holds 10 recipes.
- **Recipes from everywhere.** It's designed for saving recipes across the web, and free users get three days of tailored meal ideas each week, or the full week on Food+.
- **AI recipe tweaks.** On Food+, you can personalise a recipe: make it healthier, swap in seasonal ingredients and so on.
- **Native apps.** Samsung Food runs on Android, iPhone and the web. BiteSync runs in the browser on any device.

## Where BiteSync is stronger

- **Personal targets on the free plan.** BiteSync works out your calorie and protein targets from your weight, activity and goal, for free, and shows each day's totals as you plan. Samsung Food's free plan gives a generic daily calorie target; personal targets need Food+.
- **Plans sized to your numbers.** BiteSync Pro fills the week from your recipes. It aims for every day within 150 kcal of your calorie target, the week's average within 5%, and at least 95% of your protein, and tells you which days fall short.
- **Nutrition from the ingredients.** Recipes are calculated ingredient by ingredient, by weight, using official food composition tables. An imported recipe uses the website's own figures when it publishes them.
- **Vitamins and minerals across the week.** BiteSync Pro checks 12, such as iron, iodine and vitamin D, across the whole week against reference values split by sex, and steers the next plan towards whatever you're short on. [Why the weekly view matters](/blog/meal-planners-micronutrient-gaps).
