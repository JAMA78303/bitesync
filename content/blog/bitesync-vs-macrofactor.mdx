MacroFactor and BiteSync work on different halves of hitting your macros. MacroFactor is about **getting the targets right**. It estimates how many calories you actually burn from what you log and how your weight moves, then adjusts your targets every week. BiteSync is about **hitting the targets you have**. It plans the week's meals in advance, sized to your calories and protein, and gives you one shopping list. If you're tracking closely and want targets that adapt, choose MacroFactor. If you want the meals worked out for you, choose BiteSync.

*BiteSync is our app. We've tried to be fair about where MacroFactor is the better choice.*

## Where MacroFactor is stronger

- **Targets that learn.** MacroFactor works out your actual calorie burn from your logging and weight trend, and updates your macro targets weekly. BiteSync calculates your targets from your weight, activity and goal. They change when you update those, but they don't learn from your weight trend, and you can't type in your own numbers.
- **Fast logging.** Search, barcode scanning, nutrition-label scanning and AI-assisted logging. BiteSync has no barcode scanner; it's built around meals you plan and cook.
- **Training too.** There's a companion MacroFactor Workouts app for lifting, sold separately or as a bundle.
- **Native apps.** MacroFactor has phone apps. BiteSync is a web app that runs in your browser.

## Where BiteSync is stronger

- **It plans your meals.** MacroFactor's website doesn't describe a meal planner. BiteSync Pro fills the whole week from your recipes. It aims for every day within 150 kcal of your calorie target, the week's average within 5%, and at least 95% of your protein, and tells you which days fall short. On the free plan, each day's totals update as you place meals.
- **A shopping list.** One merged list comes from the week and leaves out what's in your pantry.
- **Vitamins and minerals across the week.** Both report micronutrients. BiteSync Pro judges 12, including iron, iodine, vitamin D and B12, across the whole week against reference values split by sex, and steers the next plan towards whatever you're short on. [Why the weekly view matters](/blog/meal-planners-micronutrient-gaps).
- **A free plan.** MacroFactor has a 7-day trial and no free tier. BiteSync's weekly planner and grocery list are free.

- **Choose MacroFactor** if you log everything you eat and want your targets to adapt to how your body actually responds, especially if you also lift and want the Workouts app.
